Definitions, limitations and source

How the figures work

Closing cartons = unloaded cartons − loaded cartons. Closing pallet equivalents = closing cartons ÷ the product’s cartons per pallet. Pallet equivalents describe quantity, not occupied locations.

Productivity = relevant output ÷ (recorded activity minutes ÷ 60). The DC carton-picking rate weights each operator by time, rather than taking a simple average of their rates.

Inbound cartons per pallet = total inbound cartons ÷ total inbound pallet equivalents. Changes can reflect product mix, rather than better pallet fill. Full-pallet picking excludes individual carton picks.

What this dataset cannot establish

One day cannot establish a trend, normal demand, excess stock, a bottleneck, paid overtime or employee capability. No actual targets, cost rates, order deadlines, stock age, expiry or storage capacity are provided. Hypothetical rate improvements indicate task capacity, not cash savings.
